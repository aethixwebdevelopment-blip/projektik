# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React + TypeScript + Vite, as specified for this greenfield MVP in the supplied project specification.

## Users
People with disabilities, including wheelchair users and people with limited mobility or limb differences, who want general fitness activities that fit their stated abilities, equipment, environment, and preferences. The public-facing Slovak copy names people with disabilities as MOVE4ALL's intended audience.

## Product Purpose
MOVE4ALL helps a person configure a movement profile, adapt a general fitness session to today's stated needs, and understand why each demo exercise was selected. Success is a usable, accessible prototype of the path from profile setup through workout feedback.

## Positioning
The intended mechanism combines hard eligibility rules, SMART SWAP alternatives, REAL LIFE MODE constraints, DAILY ADAPT temporary changes, and a plain-language explanation of each match. Do not claim novelty or clinical equivalence.

## Operating Context
Phone-first use, including one-handed operation and assistive technology. The same prototype should remain usable on wider screens for demonstration and later presentation work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The MVP is a local, interactive demo with profile setup, goals and environment, a daily check-in, deterministic eligibility and ranking, explainable swaps, exercise details, active-session controls, feedback, history, and accessibility settings. Use a small fictional exercise set visibly marked as demo and not professionally reviewed. Unknown capabilities fail closed. Production recommendations require approved content; no diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, injury-prevention claim, camera analysis, or paid API.

## Brand Commitments
Use the name MOVE4ALL and Slovak-first, respectful, direct language. The user explicitly references a black, white, and vivid cyan fitness UI and an iPhone 17 Pro presentation frame. A card-based training diary is the selected first impression.

## Evidence on Hand
The supplied MOVE4ALL specification and fitness-app montage are the only product evidence. There are no approved exercise records, clinical reviews, user-study results, testimonials, or verified product metrics. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- Adapt the session to a person's explicitly stated capabilities and preferences.
- Never infer capability from diagnosis or equipment use.
- Apply safety and eligibility filters before ranking or feedback.
- Explain each selection from actual applied rules.
- Keep the demo content visibly distinct from professionally reviewed material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large touch targets, high contrast, screen-reader labels, keyboard operation, scalable text, reduced motion, one-handed operation, text alternatives for media, and a clear way to pause or stop are required design goals.
